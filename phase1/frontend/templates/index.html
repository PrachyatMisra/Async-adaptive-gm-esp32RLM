Tokenization Advantages - Phase 1 vs Phase 2

Phase 1: DistilBERT (Paper)
  • Subword handling, OOV split
  • Pretrained alignment
  • Standard benchmark comparable
  • 96 tokens captures IMDB
  • Disadvantage: ~100KB vocab, not for MCU
Phase 2: FNV-1a Hash (ESP32)
  • Zero vocab storage (0 bytes)
  • Constant memory, streaming
  • Deterministic C/Python identical
  • Collision ~8 pairs @4096 buckets
  • Disadvantage: no subword semantics
